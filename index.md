After the great AO3calypse of 2026, many of us realised how much it matters to keep the stories that are important to us. This guide helps you build a fanfic library of your own, so you can enjoy your favourites whatever happens to the sites they live on.

<div class="callout warning">
  <div class="callout-title">Please be gentle with AO3</div>
  <p>As of November 2026, AO3 is still not back to full functionality. Please be mindful, and don't download lots of stories at once.</p>
</div>

## What you'll be able to do

- Save stories from AO3 to your own computer, as e-books you can read offline.
- Add new chapters to stories you've already saved.
- Find a story in your library by its title, author or tags.
- Read your stories on your computer, and see how to move them to a phone or e-reader.

## What you need

- **Calibre**, version 9.16 or newer. It's free, and you can download it from [calibre-ebook.com](https://calibre-ebook.com/download).
- An internet connection.
- No AO3 account, for stories that anyone can read.
- No coding. Everything happens in clicks and a little copying and pasting.

<div class="callout note">
  <div class="callout-title">Windows, Mac and Linux</div>
  <p>Calibre runs on all three. The screenshots in this guide were taken on a Mac, so on Windows some windows and menus may look slightly different. Where it matters, the steps say so.</p>
</div>

<div class="callout note">
  <div class="callout-title">Other sites</div>
  <p>This guide uses AO3 for all its examples. FanFicFare also works with many other sites, such as FanFiction.net and Royal Road, and the steps are very similar. Some of those sites need extra settings or a few tweaks, which are not covered here.</p>
</div>
