# FFF basics

<!-- No cover image yet: fff-covers has a social image here (assets/img/fff-covers-social2.png). -->

If you want to keep your favourite fanfiction safe on your own computer, but Calibre and FanFicFare sound like a lot: this guide is for you.

**FFF basics** is a beginner-friendly guide to downloading and keeping fanfiction with Calibre and FanFicFare. It starts from zero, with Calibre installed and nothing else, and ends with a small library of your own that you can read and keep up to date. It uses AO3 for every example.

> **Status:** draft. Nothing is published yet. Tested on a Mac with Calibre 9.18 and FanFicFare 4.63.0. Not yet tested on Windows.

## What's covered

Start with [What's what](whats-what.md) for a short introduction to the tools, then follow five hands-on steps in order:

1. **Add your first story to Calibre.** A tour of the Calibre window, adding a story file, reading it, and changing its details.
2. **Install FanFicFare.** Add the plugin to Calibre.
3. **Set up FanFicFare.** One line of settings, one tick-box, and two optional shortcuts for adult-rated and login-only stories.
4. **Download a story from its link.** Add a story straight from its AO3 address.
5. **Keep a story up to date.** Fetch new chapters for a story you've already downloaded.

After that, [What next?](next-steps.md) has ideas for reading on a phone or e-reader, finding and sorting stories, looking after your library, and what to explore later.

The guide is **modular**. This is the basic module. More advanced modules like anthologies, custom columns and tidying tags might come later. Automatic covers have their own guide: **[fff-covers](https://github.com/wordsandpics/fff-covers)**.
